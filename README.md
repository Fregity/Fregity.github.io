## Introduction

In **Anthony Clicker**, your goal is simple: click to earn points. Each click not only increases your score but also triggers a delightful fart sound, making the experience both entertaining and addictive. Compete with friends to see who can achieve the highest score and unlock all the secret features!
## Gameplay

- Click on Anthony to earn points.
- Each click produces a unique fart sound effect.
- Accumulate points to unlock upgrades.
- Keep clicking to increase your score and become the ultimate fart champion!

## Features

- **Hilarious Sound Effects**: Enjoy a variety of fart sounds with each click.
- **Upgrades**: Unlock upgrades to enhance your clicking power and earn points faster.
- **Leaderboard**: Compete with friends and see who can achieve the highest score.
- **Simple and Addictive Gameplay**: Easy to pick up and play, perfect for all ages.

## Installation

To run **Anthony Clicker** locally, follow these steps:

1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   https://github.com/Fregity/Fregity.github.io
   ```
2. Navigate to the project directory:
   ```bash
   cd Anthony Clicker
   ```
3. Open `anthonyClicker.html` in your web browser.

## How to Play

1. Click anywhere on the screen to start earning points.
2. Listen for the fart sound with each click!
3. Use your points to unlock upgrades.
4. Keep clicking to increase your score and have fun!
